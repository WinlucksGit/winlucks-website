Benefits

What are the benefits of selling on Winlucks?

Winlucks helps you reach a wider customer base, boost in-store visits, and increase sales through promotions and giveaways.

Get discovered

Your shop, products and offers appear to customers browsing shops nearby.

Promote offers

Create exclusive deals and discounts to attract more customers.

Host giveaways

Run daily or hourly giveaways and reward customers with loyalty points. Winlucks helps manage the process.

Track performance

See giveaway participation, sales performance and customer engagement on your dashboard.
