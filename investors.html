Investors

Invest in the future of local commerce in the UAE

Winlucks is a rewarding marketplace and e-commerce app that helps local businesses get discovered and gives shoppers more value on every purchase.

Request the investor packBusiness model
Vision

To build the region’s most rewarding shopping platform, where every order brings joy, and every store gets discovered.

The problem

Local SMEs lack affordable ways to reach nearby customers, run promotions and keep shoppers coming back, while traditional promotions take months to run and lose relevance.

Our solution

One app where shoppers discover local shops, compare offers and earn rewards, and merchants get listings, promotions, giveaways and e-commerce tools.

Why it works

Rewards, WL Coins and loyalty points give customers a reason to return, so merchants see repeat visits, not one-off sales.

Business model

Merchants pay. Shoppers join free.

Winlucks earns from the merchants it helps grow, through a mix of revenue streams that scale with merchant activity.

  • Merchant plans: from a free Basic Plan to paid plans with e-commerce features
  • Commission on sales made through the platform
  • Advertising and sponsored listings for brands and shops
  • Service fees on selected platform features
