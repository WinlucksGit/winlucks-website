FAQ · For Customers

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers!

Everything shoppers need to know about orders, payments, reward points and giveaways on Winlucks.

CustomersVendors
What is Winlucks?
Winlucks is a marketplace platform that allows customers to browse and purchase products from various local merchants while participating in exciting giveaways and promotions.
How does Winlucks work?
Customers can order products from listed merchants through the Winlucks app, collect their orders in-store, earn reward points, and enjoy giveaways and promotions.
How do I create an account on Winlucks?
You can sign up using your phone number via the Winlucks mobile app (available on iPhone, iPad and Android) or the website.
Can I use Winlucks without registering?
Browsing is possible without an account, but an account is required to place orders, take part in giveaways, and earn reward points.
How do I place an order?
Simply browse products, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Once payment is confirmed, you will receive a pickup confirmation from the merchant.
What payment methods are accepted?
Winlucks accepts all major debit and credit cards supported by Stripe in the UAE, along with other supported payment options. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
Can I cancel or modify my order?
Orders can only be cancelled within the merchant's specified cancellation window. Once confirmed, modifications are not allowed.
How do I earn reward points?
Every purchase above AED 10 from registered merchants earns you reward points. You can also collect loyalty points from your favourite shops and enjoy free giveaways hosted by merchants.
How will I know when I receive a reward?
You are notified via the Winlucks app. You can also check the rewards section in your account.
What happens if I don't claim my reward on time?
Rewards not claimed within the specified time will expire.
Can I return or exchange a product?
Returns and exchanges depend on the merchant’s policies. Check with the respective merchant before purchasing.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds for cancelled orders are processed based on the merchant’s policies. Contact the merchant if you face any issues.
Is my personal data safe with Winlucks?
Yes, we follow strict security protocols to protect your information. Your data is only used for processing orders, customer support, and promotional purposes.
Can Winlucks use my name and photo for promotions?
Yes. By using the platform, you agree that Winlucks may display your name, photo and location in promotional banners, reward announcements and merchant advertisements. If you do not agree, please do not use the app or website.
How can I contact customer support?
You can reach us by email at customersupport@winlucks.com, chat, call +971 54 31 32 357, or send us a message. We will be happy to help you!
